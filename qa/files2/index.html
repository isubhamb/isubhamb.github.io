Text Tool
Select Border Color = Text Color
When you click the Text tool, a color picker will appear. The color you choose becomes both the text color and the border color of the text box. Pick your color, then click anywhere on the PDF to place your text.
Click Text tool (or press T) — a color picker popup appears.
Choose a color from the palette — this will be your text & border color.
Click anywhere on the PDF canvas to place a text box with that color.
Type your text. Click outside or press another tool to freeze it.
You can also change the color afterwards by double-clicking the frozen text box to re-enter edit mode, then picking a new color from the right-side panel's stroke color.
